How a suit is made.

  1. Consultation

    Tell us the occasion and the look. We talk cloth, cut and colour.

  2. Measurements

    We take your measurements and note posture and fit preferences.

  3. Fittings

    We check the fit as it develops and adjust until it sits right.

  4. Delivery

    Your finished suit, pressed and ready to wear.

Photo: the tailor at work, 4:5

Clothing made around you.

Every suit starts with a conversation about how you want to look and where you will wear it. From there we choose the cloth, take your measurements and fit the suit until it is right.

Whether you choose bespoke or made to measure, you work directly with the tailor from the first consultation to the final fitting.

Before you book.

Can I choose my own cloth and lining?

Yes. The colours on this page are a preview. At your consultation you see real swatches and choose together.

What should I bring to a first fitting?

A shirt and shoes you plan to wear with the suit, and photos of any looks you like.

How long does a suit take?

It depends on whether you choose bespoke or made to measure. We confirm the timeframe and price at your consultation.
